# Freelancing Course - Task Assignments

## Course Overview

This course covers essential freelancing skills including creating your unique value proposition, understanding freelancing platforms, writing proposals, negotiation tactics, and building your freelancer profile.

---

## Assignment 1: Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

**Objective:** Create your personal USP that highlights your unique skills, experience, and value you bring to clients.

**Requirements:**

- Write a compelling English USP (2-3 sentences)
- Write an Arabic USP (2-3 sentences)
- Focus on your specific skills, experience, and what makes you different
